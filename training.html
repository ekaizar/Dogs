How the plan works

What NW2 asks for: 5 searches covering all 4 elements, with 1–3 hides per search. You're told the count, and time limits usually run 4 minutes or less. Hides go up to about 4 feet high. One container search has 4 distractors: a tennis ball or Kong plus 3 foods from at least 2 categories (fruit/veg, meat, cheese, carbs). See the NACSW rule book and container guidelines.

Weekly rhythm: Monday and Thursday evenings, Saturday mornings; Tuesday and Wednesday evenings stay free. After daylight saving time ends on Nov 1, evening sessions are containers on a lit porch or yard.

Each session: set hides 10–15 minutes before you start, give Ruby 3–5 minutes between searches, and end on a clear success. If a search falls apart, make the next one easier.

Kit: 12 plastic toolboxes for every container search, and baggies of cheese, hot dog, apple slices and crackers for distractors (all four = the “full set”). Trials also use one toy distractor; this plan practices food only. Plus odor tins, tweezers, a magnet or putty, gloves, a timer and a water bowl.

Numbering the toolboxes: every container search lists which box gets odor and which gets food. Number the boxes from where Ruby starts:

  • Line: #1 is the left end as Ruby faces the line, counting right. Boxes about 2 ft apart.
  • Grid (12): 3 rows of 4, about 3 ft apart each way. Front row #1–4 left to right, middle #5–8, back #9–12, so #5 sits behind #1.
  • Grid or 2 rows (10): front row #1–5, back row #6–10, about 3 ft apart. 2 rows (12): front #1–6, back #7–12.
  • Circle: #1 is the box closest to the start line, counting clockwise.
  • Blind searches: the box list is folded under “Helper setup”. Have a helper set the hides and keep it closed yourself.
  • Boxes not listed stay empty. Use a fresh glove for each food.
